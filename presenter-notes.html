Presenter Notes

Piano Recital — St. Helen's RC Primary School — 23rd March 2026
Welcome & Introduction

Good afternoon everyone, and welcome to St. Helen's piano recital. Thank you all for coming along this afternoon — it's lovely to see so many parents and families here.

Today's concert represents two terms of hard work and dedication from every one of these performers. Some of our students started from the very beginning this year, learning where to place their hands on the keys for the first time, while others have been building on skills they already had. Every single one of them has put in the practice, and I'm really proud of how far they've all come.

Before we begin, I'd just ask that you hold your applause until each performer has finished their piece, so that everyone can play with confidence. Right, let's get started.

Closing

And that brings us to the end of today's recital. A huge well done to every single performer — you've all been brilliant, and you should be very proud of yourselves.

I'd also like to thank you, the parents and families, for all the support you give at home. Whether that's reminding them to practise, listening to the same piece for the twentieth time, or simply encouraging them to keep going — it really does make a difference, and we couldn't do this without you.

Thank you all for coming this afternoon, and well done once again to our pianists.
